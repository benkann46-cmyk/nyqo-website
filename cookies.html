Cookies

As of November 2026

This website sets no cookies

None. Not for statistics, not for advertising, not for “improving your experience”. This website also doesn't use local storage, tracking pixels or any third-party services, and it loads fonts and scripts from our own server only.

Under § 25 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG), a website needs your consent before it stores information on your device or reads it from there, unless that is strictly necessary. Since we store nothing, there is nothing to consent to, and that's why you don't see a cookie banner.

How the page knows your system

Your browser tells every website which operating system it runs on. The page reads that in your browser to point out which version of the demo fits your computer. Nothing is stored and nothing is sent to us for this.

The NYQO app

The app itself keeps your sign-in session and your encryption keys on your device, in your system's keychain and an encrypted local database. Without them you couldn't stay signed in or read your messages, so this is strictly necessary. Details are in the privacy policy.

If this ever changes

If we ever add something that needs your consent, we will ask first and update this page.
