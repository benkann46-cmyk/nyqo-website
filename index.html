A real standard

NYQO uses MLS (Messaging Layer Security, RFC 9420), the IETF standard for encrypted group chats, through the open-source OpenMLS library. No homemade cryptography.

Keys stay with you

Your keys live on your devices. To add a new one, you compare a short code on both screens or enter your recovery code.

Reporting is your call

If someone crosses a line, you can report a message. Only then is that one message shared, by you, with the people who review reports.

Straight talk: to deliver messages, the server does know some things: your account, which squads you're in, and when messages are sent. It never knows what they say.

Fair questions

What does NYQO cost?

The demo costs €3, once. NYQO Flash is an optional subscription on top for extras like nicknames and a coloured name.

What is the activation code?

Every purchase comes with one activation code. You enter it when you create your NYQO account; it works exactly once. Your account then runs on that one computer only, so it can't be shared with friends.

I got logged out or have a new PC. What now?

Contact us on our Discord with your username. You get a new code that only works for your account. When you use it, your account moves to the new computer, and the old one is signed out once the new one is set up. With your recovery code your messages come along too.

How do I buy the demo?

On the buy page: enter your email, pay €3 with PayPal or by card, and you get your activation code and the download by email.

I paid but didn't get my code. What now?

Check your spam folder first. If the code isn't there either, open a ticket on our Discord or email app@nyqo.net. Send proof of payment along: for a card payment the payment number from the receipt we emailed you or a screenshot of the charge, for PayPal a screenshot of the payment in your PayPal app.

Can you read my messages?

No. Messages, files and voice notes are encrypted on your devices with keys we never get. Our server only stores and forwards data it can't open.

What if I lose my laptop?

When you sign up, NYQO shows you a recovery code. Keep it somewhere safe. With it you can set up NYQO on a new device and get back into your squads.

Messages from before can't be decrypted on the new device. That's the price of encryption that actually holds.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows warns about apps that aren't code-signed yet, and the demo isn't. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway”. Signed builds come with the full release.
