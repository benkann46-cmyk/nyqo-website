4. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not store anything in your browser: no cookies, no local storage, no tracking pixels. That's why there is no cookie banner. The page works out your operating system from information your browser sends anyway, to point out the right version of the demo; that never leaves your browser. The buttons to buy the demo are plain links: nothing is sent to Discord until you click one. More on the cookies page.

5. Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address and what you write to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your message relates to a contract with us, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our interest in answering you). We delete the correspondence once it is settled and no retention obligations apply.

Buying the demo

When you order the demo we store your email address, your order number, the time of the order and whether it was paid, and send you the order confirmation and your activation code by email. If you pay by card, we also email you a receipt with a payment number and store that number with your order, so we can find your payment if you contact us. You pay via PayPal (PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A., Luxembourg) or by card on the payment page of Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland); each processes your payment data under its own privacy policy. Your card details go only to Stripe; we see your name, email address and whether the payment succeeded. We use this to sell and deliver the demo and keep payment records as tax law requires. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) and (c) GDPR. If you contact us on our Discord (Discord Inc., USA), Discord's privacy policy applies.

6. Using the NYQO app

This section covers the NYQO app and the NYQO server it connects to. The server is operated by [operator / hosting provider and location of the NYQO server].

Your account

To create an account we store your email address, username and display name. Your password is never stored, only a salted hash of it (Argon2). Optional profile details such as a nickname in a squad are stored when you set them. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (providing the service).

Messages, files and voice notes

Content is encrypted on your device using the MLS protocol (RFC 9420) before it is sent. Our server stores and forwards it in encrypted form only and has no key to decrypt it. Voice calls are encrypted between the devices taking part.

What the server does see

To deliver messages, the server processes metadata: which account and device a message comes from, which squad, channel or direct conversation it belongs to, when it was sent and how large it is, as well as band membership, roles, read status and online status. Your devices' public keys are stored so others can encrypt for you. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

Security and abuse protection

We process IP addresses and sign-in attempts to limit request rates and block password guessing. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is protecting accounts and the service.

Reports

If you report a message, your app sends us a decrypted copy of that message so we can review it, together with information that lets us verify the copy is genuine. This only happens when you choose to report. Reports are stored encrypted and only accessed to review them. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is keeping NYQO safe.

Translation

Translation of the interface and of chat messages runs entirely on your device. When you turn translation on, the app downloads the translation model once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co, Hugging Face, Inc., USA). Like any download, this shares your IP address with Hugging Face. No message content is sent anywhere for translation.

NYQO Flash (once available)

NYQO Flash is not on sale yet. When it is, payments will be processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland. Stripe receives the data needed for payment; we do not receive your full card details. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. This section will be completed before sales start.

Data on your device

The app stores your sign-in session and your encryption keys on your device (in your system's keychain and an encrypted local database). This is necessary for the app to work.
